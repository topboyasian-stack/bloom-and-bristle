About & policies

Everything you'd want to know before ordering — who we are, how delivery works, and what happens if something's wrong.

About Bloom & Bristle

Bloom & Bristle is a Nigeria-based online store bringing hand-picked, hard-to-find pieces to your door — from self-care tools to home gadgets — without the stress of importing them yourself.

We're a small, growing operation. Every order is currently reviewed and confirmed personally before it ships, which is why we ask for a quick WhatsApp confirmation after payment — it's the same person on the other end, not a call centre.

Shipping & delivery

  • Nationwide delivery across Nigeria, typically 3–10 business days depending on your location and the item
  • You'll receive a tracking update via WhatsApp once your order ships
  • Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees — remote areas may take longer

Returns & refunds

  • If your item arrives damaged or significantly different from what was described, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with a photo
  • Approved returns are refunded to your original payment method via Paystack, or replaced — your choice
  • Change-of-mind returns aren't currently supported while we're a small operation — please check product details carefully before ordering

Frequently asked questions

Is my payment secure?

Yes — all payments are processed directly through Paystack, a licensed Nigerian payment processor. We never see or store your card details.

Why do I need to confirm on WhatsApp after paying?

It's how we collect your delivery address and confirm order details with a real person — pricing is fixed on the site and isn't negotiated in chat.

Can I pay on delivery?

Not currently — all orders are paid upfront through Paystack to keep pricing and fulfillment simple while we're growing.

Some products say "studio photos coming soon" — is the item real?

Yes. Pricing on those items is either confirmed against a live supplier listing or a careful estimate — we just haven't finished our own product photography yet. You're welcome to ask us for more detail on WhatsApp before ordering.

Fastest way to reach us is WhatsApp — we usually respond within a few hours.
